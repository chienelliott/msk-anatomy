Compartment rules

Each compartment has one nerve, one main artery and one shared job. Learn these 13 rules and the exceptions, and you have the innervation of nearly every muscle.

The exceptions examiners love
  • Two nerves: brachialis (musculocutaneous ± radial), FDP (AIN lateral half, ulnar medial half), FPB (median superficial head, ulnar deep head), adductor magnus (obturator + tibial), pectineus (femoral ± obturator), biceps femoris (long head tibial, short head common fibular).
  • Wrong neighborhood: brachioradialis is radial but flexes the elbow; FCU is the only ulnar muscle in the superficial flexors; lumbricals 1–2 are median while every other deep hand muscle is ulnar.
  • Pierced muscles: musculocutaneous → coracobrachialis; median → between the pronator teres heads; deep radial → supinator.
